## Glossary ##

# Agent Confidence Levels #
Agent findings provide a confidence level indicating the level of evidence for the recommendation. 

Note: The following confidence levels are common for all agents.

- High: well supported by multiple, independent signals in the findings (for example, repeated occurrence, corroboration with analysis; clean and complete data). Customers can rely on it with minimal exceptions.
- Medium: partially supported (for example, fewer occurrences, limited corroboration, or minor data gaps). While informative, it should be considered alongside other evidence.
- Low: evidence is weak or conflicting (for example, single occurrence, poor data quality, ambiguous classification). This is flagged for human review rather than guessed.

Note: If an agent returns a low-confidence level, the recommendation is referred to a human (HITL) for further review.

----------


### Alert ###
An alert is a single instance of suspicious or unusual behaviour related to a customer, account or transaction in the financial services system. An alert is the first indication to investigators that suspicious activity may have occurred and is typically the first step in the investigation process.


----------


### AUSTRAC ###
The Australian Transaction Reports and Analysis Centre.
This is Australia’s financial intelligence unit and national anti–money laundering (AML) regulator.


----------


### BSA ###
A Bank Secrecy Act report, submitted to FinCEN by a financial institution that must report under the BSA.
The report is filed through the FinCEN E-Filing system by uploading a file - typically a batch XML file.


----------


### Counterparty ###
In financial services, the other party to the subject in a transaction with each being a counterparty to the other.


----------


### FinCEN ###
The Financial Crimes Enforcement Network is a bureau of the United States Department of the Treasury and is the United States’ financial intelligence unit (FIU). It safeguards financial systems from money laundering, terrorist financing and other financial crime with AML and CFT regulations.


----------


### Human-In-The-Loop (HITL) ###
Our agents use HITL as gateways in the decision-making process.Human analysts share responsibility with the agent for decisions and outputs, with the ultimate decision made by the human.


----------


### Orchestrator Agent ###
An orchestrator coordinates one or more worker agents as parallel workflows to produce a unified investigation narrative and recommendation. A human analyst (HIITL) reviews and approves recommendations before any action or decision as agents require explicit human oversight.

Note: Any low confidence findings are automatically flagged to a human reviewer.

----------


### SMR File ###
In the AUSTRAC system, a Suspicious Matter Report (SMR) is submitted (electronically) by a reporting entity when it forms a suspicion that a customer, service or transaction may be linked to money laundering, terrorism financing, or other serious crime.


----------


### Subject ###
The subject of investigation within our applications.This is typically a personal entity in a financial services system such as a customer, counterparty or business.


----------


### Transaction Monitoring ###
This solution is used to monitor transactions as part of anti-money laundering (AML) detections. Money laundering is the conversion of funds derived from illegal activities that appear to have a legal source.


----------


### Worker Agent ###
A worker agent is a specialist workflow that performs focused analysis on a single dimension - for example, transactions, counterparties; alerts or geography - returning summaries designed for integration and analyst review.

A worker agent typically focuses on a single domain - for example, weekly trends, channel or type distributions; counterparty concentration or alert timelines One or more worker agents can be coordinated by an orchestrator agent.

In the human-in-the-loop (HITL) process, worker agents do not make decisions or perform actions; findings are provided to human analysts or an orchestrator, and low confidence assessments are similarly flagged rather than guessed.
